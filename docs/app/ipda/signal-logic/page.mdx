# Signal Logic

The ported signal comes from the TradingView Pine v5 indicator (`file.txt` Section 5).

## Supertrend + SMA

```
supertrend(close, sensitivity=14, atrLen=11)     # ATR is Wilder/RMA-smoothed
sma = ta.sma(close, 13)
bull = ta.crossover(close, supertrend)  and close >= sma   -> BUY
bear = ta.crossunder(close, supertrend) and close <= sma   -> SELL
```

| Side | Condition |
|------|-----------|
| **BUY** | Close crosses **above** Supertrend **and** close ≥ SMA(13) |
| **SELL** | Close crosses **below** Supertrend **and** close ≤ SMA(13) |

Service defaults: `SUPERTREND_SENSITIVITY=14`, `SUPERTREND_ATR_LEN=11`, `SMA_LEN=13`,
`TARGET_TF_MINUTES=5`.

`atrLen=11` and the SMA length of 13 are hardcoded in the Pine call and must not be changed —
the Pine source names that SMA `sma9` while its length is 13. Only `SUPERTREND_SENSITIVITY`
corresponds to a Pine input. Match the chart to **5M** and Sensitivity **14** — see
[Pine Script](/ipda/pine).

### Default exits (hard targets)

With `USE_HARD_TARGETS=true` (the shipped env default), SL/TP are fixed pip distances:

- `STOP_LOSS_PIPS=40`, `TAKE_PROFIT_PIPS=50` on forex
- Distances travel to mt5-trader as `stop_loss_distance` / `take_profit_distance` so the
  broker anchors them to the fill price

**`RISK_REWARD` is inert** while hard targets are on. With `USE_HARD_TARGETS=false`, the stop
becomes the Supertrend line at signal time and the target becomes `RISK_REWARD` × that
distance (absolute prices). See [Hard Targets](/ipda/hard-targets).

## No confluence

Unlike [LuxAlgo](/lux-algo/confluence), IPDA does **not** gate entries with Range Filter,
SuperIchi, TBO, Smart Trail, or other overlays, and it has no counter-trend vetoes. A Supertrend
× SMA cross is the full trigger — then the [session gate](/ipda/sessions) decides whether to
execute or only notify.

## Mid-candle policy: fire once, then lock

A signal can appear on any poll while a target candle is still forming and may change before it
closes (repainting). This service:

1. Emits the **first** signal seen for a bucket
2. Then **locks** that bucket — at most one entry per target candle

The committed signal reflects the forming bar at fire time and is **never retracted**.
TradingView's alert on the same indicator uses `alert.freq_once_per_bar_close`, so it only
confirms at the close. A label that appears two minutes into a 5M bar and disappears before the
bar closes still produces a live trade here.

```
TARGET_TF bucket (5 minutes)
│
├─ poll t=0:00  forming bar ── no signal
├─ poll t=0:15  forming bar ── BUY fires  ──▶ lock bucket, session check, submit or notify
├─ poll t=0:30  forming bar ── would flip? ── ignored (locked)
└─ poll t=0:45  … until next bucket (:05)
```

Keep `POLL_INTERVAL_SECONDS` **&lt; 60s** so mid-candle firing can occur within the forming bar.

<Callout type="warning">
  Mid-candle fire-once-lock means a signal that would have disappeared by bar close still gets
  submitted (when in session). Expect divergence vs a chart that only marks closed-bar signals.
</Callout>

## Deterministic `signal_id` (UUIDv5)

The `signal_id` is a deterministic **UUIDv5** of `(symbol, bucket start, direction)`, keyed as
`ipda:{symbol}:{bucket}:{direction}`.

A transport retry **replays idempotently** against mt5-trader instead of creating a duplicate or
a `409 idempotency_conflict`. Idempotency is per `(symbol, bucket, direction)` — multiple
simultaneous signals on different instruments do not conflict.
