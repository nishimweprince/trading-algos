Quotes

Fair = Kalshi YES mid. Our bids: Up at fair − h − skew, Down at 1 − (fair + h − skew), post-only, 1¢ ticks. Capture = fair at fill − price; markouts = Kalshi's mid 5 s / 30 s later − price (negative = adverse selection); 2 min and 5 min stop at the close, and "at close" is Kalshi's mid 1 s before it. Pair guard: with shares held on one side, the other side bids at most 1 − their average cost − 2¢, so every completed pair locks ≥ 2¢. Uptime = share of the window with a bid resting.
