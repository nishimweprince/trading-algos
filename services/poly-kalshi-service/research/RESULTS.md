- **Null battery** (signal − `random_same_rule`): +3.82 pp in discovery, **+5.82 pp in the holdout**, +4.2 pp in the earlier regime. The signal is real, and it lives below 0.50: the excess is +8 to +11¢ in the 0.0–0.5 bands and ≤ 0 above 0.6.
- **Latency, price side:** at 150 ms only **47–53%** of entries still find the ask at or below the limit. Epoch 3 measured 57% live. The first 50 ms costs 27 points of fill and 1.9¢ per entry; each 50 ms after that costs 7–10 points and 0.5–0.7¢.
- **Adverse selection** is confirmed over 39 days. Entries we would miss at 150 ms net +7.6¢ at the touch; those we get net +4.6¢. In the July spot regime 79% still filled at 150 ms and the gap was absent, so the competition for these fills arrived over the summer.
- **Trade-through proxy:** a buy at or through our ask before our order does not predict the outcome (win rate 48.8% vs 48.5%). It is not usable as an adverse-selection measure.
- **Polymarket spread at the crossing:** above 0.10 the entry loses (−2.3¢); at 1¢ or less it makes +9.1¢. This is a candidate filter for the next pre-registration.
- **Capacity:** the median $ at the touch is ~$5 (BTC ~$14). On our tape with full depth, **$9.8 is filled per $50 entry at 150 ms**, $16.4 at +2¢ and $20.0 at +3¢. Even at 0 ms only 31% of the order fills. Depth binds before latency does.
- **Sizing** (base, one share vs $50 notional vs min($50, touch)): min($50, touch) earns 15% of the $50 P&L. The $50 figure is not reachable.

## Phase 4: a faster signal

- **4.2 lead and lag** (08-20 and 09-01, 20 ms bins, inside entry windows):
  - Kalshi → Polymarket peaks at 0–40 ms on every asset, with 27–44% of the positive mass beyond 150 ms.
  - Polymarket's next 500 ms is explained by Kalshi's last second (R² 0.01–0.08). **Adding spot adds nothing** (BTC 09-01: 0.0781 → 0.0789). Spot alone explains 4–10× less.
  - The spot available here is Binance's 100 ms depth stream. It **trails Kalshi by 40–60 ms**, so this tape cannot say how fast real spot is. Chainlink trails Binance by ~2.2 s.
- **4.3 fair value** (Chainlink, K = TWAP at the open, the TWAP-averaging variance, σ over 120/300/900 s):
  - It **loses at every edge**: −1.7 to −2.5¢ (day-t −2.6 to −6.3), BTC included.
  - It is less calibrated than either venue's mid: Brier 0.164 against Polymarket 0.162 and Kalshi 0.160.
  - On the same per-second grid the Kalshi gap makes +6.2¢ (day-t 9.8; BTC +8.1¢).

**Decision:** on history, a fair-value signal from the settlement oracle does not beat the gap, and spot as recorded here adds nothing to Kalshi. Whether *fast* spot leads Polymarket by more than 150 ms is still open and needs 4.1 (a low-latency spot feed recorded in Dublin). Until then BTC stays paper-only:
- holdout base −0.2¢ at the touch (n = 317);
- on our tape it fills 84% at 150 ms for $32, and nets −0.9¢.

## Phase 5: decisions

- **5.1 location:** stay in Dublin. Benchmark a provider (AWS eu-west-1) only if fills at 150 ms are worth chasing. On this tape, past 50 ms, each 50 ms is 7–10 points of fill and 0.5–0.7¢ per entry, so 2 ms is worth little next to depth. The benchmark itself needs the Dublin host.
- **5.2 Kalshi relay:** not worth it. Kalshi leads Polymarket by tens of ms, and the relay saves 0–5 ms at best.
- **5.3 rewrite:** not triggered. The engine costs 0.04 ms. Phase 2 acceptance on Dublin decides the gateway.
