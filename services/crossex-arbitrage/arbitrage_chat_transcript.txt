Assistant: Binance to Bybit cross-platform arbitrage is viable in theory, but difficult and low-margin in practice due to high market efficiency and automated trading bots.

How It Works:
* Spatial Arbitrage: You buy a cryptocurrency (like BTC or altcoins) on Binance where the price is lower, and simultaneously sell it on Bybit where the price is higher (or vice versa).
* Typical Spreads: Price discrepancies between Binance and Bybit usually range from 0.1% to 0.5% during normal conditions, though they can widen slightly during high market volatility.

Key Viability Factors & Challenges:
* Trading & Maker/Taker Fees: Binance charges roughly 0.02% maker / 0.05% taker, and Bybit charges 0.02% maker / 0.055% taker for regular users. Round-trip trading fees eat into a large chunk of a 0.3% spread.
* Transfer Speeds & Network Costs: Moving crypto between exchanges takes time. If you transfer on-chain (e.g., via TRC-20 or BEP-20), you must account for network withdrawal fees and confirmation delays. By the time the asset arrives, the price gap often closes.
* High Competition from Bots: Most manual arbitrage opportunities are instantly captured by high-frequency trading (HFT) bots and automated market makers.
* Slippage and Liquidity: While both platforms have deep liquidity for major pairs like BTC/USDT, larger order sizes cause slippage that can turn a theoretical profit into a net loss.

Is It Worth It?
* For Manual Traders: Generally not profitable for small or medium balances once you factor in fees, transfer time, and slippage.
* For Automated/API Traders: Viable only if you run low-latency bots, hold VIP fee tiers on both exchanges, and focus on high-volatility events or less-liquid altcoin pairs rather than major pairs like BTC/USDT.

import asyncio
import ccxt.pro as ccxtpro

# Configuration
SYMBOL = 'RENDER/USDT'  # Example mid-cap asset
BINANCE_TAKER_FEE = 0.00075  # 0.075% (Assumes BNB discount enabled)
BYBIT_TAKER_FEE = 0.00100    # 0.100% (Standard API Taker fee)

async def watch_binance():
    exchange = ccxtpro.binance({'enableRateLimit': True})
    while True:
        try:
            # Streams real-time order book ticker data
            ticker = await exchange.watch_ticker(SYMBOL)
            return {
                'bid': float(ticker['bid']),
                'ask': float(ticker['ask']),
                'timestamp': ticker['timestamp']
            }
        except Exception as e:
            await asyncio.sleep(1)

async def watch_bybit():
    exchange = ccxtpro.bybit({'enableRateLimit': True})
    while True:

            ticker = await exchange.watch_ticker(SYMBOL)

                'bid': float(ticker['bid']),
