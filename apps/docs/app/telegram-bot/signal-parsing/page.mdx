# Signal Parsing

Parser **v1** (locked in `PLAN.md`):

- Matches **Gold / XAU** phrases plus **buy|sell** within ~40 characters
- **Ambiguous** messages that contain both buy and sell are **rejected**

<Callout type="info">
  Rejected ambiguity is logged under `logs/errors.jsonl` (parser ambiguity).
  Successful parses go to `logs/signals.jsonl`.
</Callout>

Level extraction, per-channel parser configs, and richer instruments are
[out of scope](/telegram-bot/out-of-scope) for v1.
