# Bitcoin 9to5

**Bitcoin 9to5** is a BTC perpetual futures trading bot for [Nado](https://nado.xyz).

**Premise:** BTC tends to drop during US market hours and rise overnight. The bot exploits this
by shorting 9-to-5 and going long the rest of the time.

## Zones at a glance

| Zone | When (ET) | Direction |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| **Short** | 9:29 AM – 4:01 PM weekdays | Short |
| **Long** | Overnight, weekends, and holidays | Long |

At each zone change, the bot **flips** — closing any existing position and opening a new one in
the zone's direction. Times can auto-adjust via [adaptive learning](/bitcoin9to5/adaptive).

```
Long (overnight) ──▶ Short 9:29 ET ──▶ Long 4:01 ET ──▶ …
                         flip              flip
```

**Profit-taking:** When price moves **1%** in your favor, the bot closes for profit (~10% gain at
10x leverage). Longs can enter a trailing **TP zone** instead of closing immediately — see
[Profit Taking](/bitcoin9to5/profit-taking).

<Callout type="warning">
  This bot requires an Ethereum **`PRIVATE_KEY`** and trades **live** on Nado with leverage.
  Keep the key out of version control, restrict who can access the host, and understand that
  perpetual futures can lose more than collateral under adverse moves. Nothing here is financial
  advice.
</Callout>
